## Data We Collect

When you contact us or engage our services, we collect:

- **Contact information**: name, email, phone, company
- **Service inquiry details** and project information
- **Communication records** related to service delivery
- **Business information** necessary for consulting and business setup

## How We Use Your Data

- To provide requested services and communicate about projects
- To send service updates, reports, and relevant information
- To improve our services and website experience
- To coordinate with authorities in business setup processes (with your consent)

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share information with:

- **Service providers** necessary for project delivery (with your consent)
- **Licensed professionals** (brokers, banks, legal advisors, designers) when coordinating regulated services
- **UAE authorities** when required for business setup processes
- **Authorities** when required by law

## Your Rights (GDPR Compliance)

If you are in the EU, you have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Request correction or deletion
- Withdraw consent for processing
- Request data portability

Contact us at **info@bestvisual.ae** to exercise these rights.

## Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. Data is stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel.

This website uses essential cookies for functionality and analytics cookies to understand site usage. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.
