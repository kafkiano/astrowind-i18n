## Why a Free Zone?

UAE Free Zones offer 100% foreign ownership, zero corporate tax (for qualifying activities), full repatriation of profits, and no currency restrictions. For international service businesses, consultancies, and trading companies, a Free Zone is often the most efficient structure.

The trade license itself can be issued in **3 to 7 working days** once your documentation is complete. But full operational readiness — including visa processing and corporate bank account opening — realistically takes **4 to 8 weeks**.

## The 6-Week Timeline

### Week 1: Define Your Setup

- Choose your Free Zone (RAKEZ, DMCC, IFZA, Meydan, etc.)
- Select your legal structure (FZE, FZC, or branch)
- Define your business activities (typically 1–10 related activities per license)
- Confirm office requirements (flexi-desk, shared, or physical office)

### Week 2: Document Preparation

- Passport copies (shareholders, managers)
- Visa or entry stamp copy
- Passport-sized photographs
- Business plan (required by some Free Zones)
- Specimen signature forms
- No-objection certificate if currently employed in UAE

### Week 3: Application & Payment

- Submit application to the Free Zone authority
- Pay license and registration fees
- Receive initial approval (typically 2–5 working days)

### Week 4: License Issuance

- Trade license issued
- Establishment card issued
- Lease agreement finalized (flexi-desk or office)
- Share certificates issued

### Weeks 5–6: Visa & Banking

- Entry permit application
- Medical fitness test
- Emirates ID application
- Visa stamping in passport
- Corporate bank account application (can take 2–4 weeks independently)

## What Most Timelines Miss

The trade license is fast. The bottleneck is almost always the **corporate bank account**. UAE banks have strict compliance requirements, and account opening can take 2–6 weeks depending on the bank, your nationality, and your business activity.

**Practical tip**: Start the bank account process as soon as your trade license is issued. Do not wait until visas are complete.
